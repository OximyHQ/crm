# Oximy product context

This document gives sellers a stable starting point.

Confirm customer-specific claims before using them in outreach.

## Visibility

### Product

Visibility shows how employees and agents use AI across an organization.

It attributes tools, models, accounts, agents, activity, and spend.

It combines network observation, first-party integrations, and optional endpoint depth.

Product page: https://www.oximy.com/visibility

### ICP

- Organizations with broad or growing AI adoption.
- Security, IT, and finance teams that lack a reliable AI inventory.
- Leaders that need adoption, risk, and cost evidence before expanding AI programs.

### Buyer personas

- CIO and CTO.
- CISO and security leadership.
- IT operations and enterprise architecture leadership.
- Finance and procurement leadership for AI spend control.

### Problems

- Teams cannot see every AI tool or account in use.
- Leaders cannot connect AI activity to users, teams, models, or cost.
- Security teams cannot govern unknown AI usage.
- Finance teams cannot explain fragmented AI spend.

### Objections

- Existing SaaS management tools already show approved applications.
- Endpoint deployment creates operational concern.
- Employees can interpret monitoring as surveillance.
- Leaders do not yet have a formal AI governance program.

### Pitch guidance

- Lead with the buyer's missing AI inventory and decision evidence.
- Explain the available observation depths without overstating coverage.
- Connect visibility to governance, adoption, or spend based on the buyer's role.
- Avoid fear-based shadow AI language without specific evidence.

## Relay

### Product

Relay routes AI requests across providers using cost, quality, and latency goals.

Teams can use gateway, route-only, and observe or shadow deployment modes.

Product page: https://www.oximy.com/relay

### ICP

- Product and platform teams that use several models or providers.
- AI applications with material inference cost, latency, or reliability constraints.
- Engineering teams that want routing control without rebuilding model selection logic.

### Buyer personas

- CTO and VP Engineering.
- AI platform and infrastructure leadership.
- Product engineering leadership.
- FinOps leadership for large inference workloads.

### Problems

- One model does not provide the best result for every request.
- Model changes create application work and operational risk.
- Teams trade cost, quality, and latency through static configuration.
- Provider incidents or limits reduce application reliability.

### Objections

- The team already built basic routing.
- A new gateway adds latency or reliability concern.
- The workload is too small for routing gains.
- Model quality is difficult to measure automatically.

### Pitch guidance

- Start with the current routing decision and its operational cost.
- Quantify request volume, model mix, latency targets, and spend before claiming value.
- Match the deployment mode to the team's control and migration needs.
- Position Relay as routing infrastructure, not another model provider.

## Sidekick

### Product

Sidekick is a company-owned AI workbench for employees and agents.

It provides approved models, connectors, skills, keys, governance, budgets, and audit controls.

Product page: https://www.oximy.com/sidekick

### ICP

- Organizations that want one governed AI work surface.
- Teams that use several assistants without shared company context or controls.
- Leaders that want employees and agents to use approved models and data connections.

### Buyer personas

- CIO and CTO.
- AI transformation and digital workplace leadership.
- IT and security leadership.
- Business operations leaders scaling repeatable AI workflows.

### Problems

- Employees switch between disconnected AI tools.
- Company context, connectors, and workflows do not travel between assistants.
- Teams lack shared controls for models, keys, budgets, and audit.
- Useful prompts remain personal and inconsistent.

### Objections

- Employees already use ChatGPT or Claude.
- Another work surface creates adoption concern.
- Existing assistants already support connectors and skills.
- Central controls can reduce individual flexibility.

### Pitch guidance

- Lead with the missing company-owned layer across models, data, and workflows.
- Show how approved skills make repeatable work easier for employees.
- Position governance as an enabler for wider adoption.
- Compare the complete workflow with existing assistants, not chat quality alone.
